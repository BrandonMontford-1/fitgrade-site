SUPPORT

Need help with FitGrade? Find answers to common questions below or reach out directly — we respond within 24 hours.

How do I add clients to my roster?

From your Trainer Home screen, tap "Add Client" to generate a unique join code. Share the code with your athlete — they enter it in the app and link to your roster instantly.

How does the AI training plan work?

Select a client, tap "Generate Plan," and answer a short intake — sport, position, fitness level, injuries, and goals. FitGrade's AI builds a personalized 7-day program in seconds.

What's included in the free tier?

The free tier includes AI plan generation, client management (up to 3 clients), session logging, and injury tracking. Upgrade to Pro to unlock unlimited clients and advanced features.

How do I upgrade to Pro?

Go to Settings → Upgrade to Pro inside the app. Billing is handled securely through Apple's in-app purchase system.

I forgot my password — how do I reset it?

On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password." Enter your email address and we'll send you a reset link within a few minutes.

How do I log a session?

Navigate to a client's profile and tap "Log Session." Select the date, add workout notes, and mark completion. All sessions are saved to the client's history.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This action is permanent and removes all your data from our servers.
